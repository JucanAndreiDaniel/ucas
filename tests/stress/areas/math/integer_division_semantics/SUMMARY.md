## Takeaway
Core constant and identity semantics of `/` and `%` are correct (including wrapping constants and magic-number division), but `find` hangs on 16-bit inexact remainder, `rem`/`quo` on integer constants and the unsampled `int` range return plainly wrong answers, and the English frontend mixes floor and truncating division conventions.
