Probe: 2048 warps on SMs [0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5] (gl_SMCountNV 6, gl_WarpsPerSMNV 64, highest warp slot 63).
fadd: 0.001 ns a lane-step, 3.90 warp instructions a clock an SM (sm_61 peak 4).
